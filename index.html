Our story

Designed by a woman,
for every woman.

Nohé started with a sketchbook and a simple idea: clothes should make you feel like the most at-ease version of yourself. No noise, no trends that expire in a month — just pieces you reach for again and again.

Each design begins with the way it moves, then the fabric, then the small details — a covered button, a softened seam, a pocket exactly where your hand falls.

— With love, the Nohé studio
